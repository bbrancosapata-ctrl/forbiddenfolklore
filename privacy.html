Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Forbidden Folklore Uploader ("the tool") is a private desktop tool used only by Brayan Branco Sapata, owner of the Forbidden Folklore channels on YouTube (@ForbiddenFolkloreOfficial) and TikTok (@forbiddenfolklore). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

YouTube API Services: uploads the owner's videos to the owner's own channel and reads the connected channel's name and ID.

TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): reads basic profile information of the owner's own account (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and uploads/publishes the owner's own videos to that account.

The tool does not access, collect or process data from any other user.

How data is stored

Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Data can be deleted at any time by deleting the local token files and revoking access.

Revoking access

Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions. TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. The tool uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply, as well as the TikTok Privacy Policy.
